I'm sorry for the moments when my words or actions made you feel hurt, unimportant, misunderstood, or emotionally tired. I'm sorry for the times I reacted when I should have understood, explained myself when I should have listened, or let my own emotions get in the way of what you were feeling.

I don't want this to be an apology written only because I'm scared of losing you. I want it to be an apology because I genuinely understand that you deserved better from me in those moments.

I can't go back and change those moments. But I can take responsibility for them. I can learn. I can listen better. I can think before I speak. And I can show you through my actions that your feelings matter to me.

I know trust and comfort aren't rebuilt by one beautiful paragraph. They are rebuilt by consistency, patience and actions. So I don't want to make huge promises just for the sake of this birthday. I simply want to become better — genuinely, slowly, and for real.

I'm truly sorry for every time I hurt the person I care about so much. ❤️
